Process

Four steps

  1. Brief

    You send the subject, the audience and where the film will be shown.

  2. Script

    I write the script. You approve it before any design starts.

  3. Style frame

    You see one finished frame and approve the look.

  4. Film

    The finished film with voice-over, captions and sound, in the sizes you need.
